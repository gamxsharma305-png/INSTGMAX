Shipping / Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Digital service only — no physical shipping.
GMAX Hub does not ship physical products, parcels, or goods by courier.

1. Nature of delivery

All purchases on this website are for digital subscription access (online content / feed access for a selected plan and profile). There is no physical delivery address required for product shipment.

2. How digital access is delivered

  • Online payments (for example Razorpay): After successful payment confirmation, access is activated on the user’s device/session for the relevant profile, usually immediately or within a short processing time.
  • Manual UPI / UTR verification: Access is activated after our team verifies the payment reference, which may take reasonable working time.

3. Delivery timeline

Successful online payments are generally fulfilled instantly or within a few minutes. In rare cases (network issues, gateway delay, or verification needs), delivery of access may take longer. If access is not received after a successful payment, contact us with your payment reference.

4. No shipping charges

Because there is no physical shipment, no shipping or courier charges apply.

5. Service area

The service is delivered online via the internet. You need a compatible device and network connection to use the website.

6. Contact

For delivery/access issues, see Contact Us and share your name, email, plan, and payment reference.
