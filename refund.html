Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

7-day refund policy: Genuine issues within 7 days of successful payment approval/access can be considered for refund.

1. Nature of product

GMAX Hub sells digital subscription access. Once access is delivered, the product is considered provided.

2. 7-day refund window

Valid examples within 7 days:

  • Payment deducted but access not received after verification
  • Technical issue on our side blocking paid access
  • Duplicate payment for the same order

3. How to request

Contact us via Contact Us with name, email/mobile, UTR, plan, date, and issue details.

4. Review

Valid requests within 7 days will be reviewed and refunded to the original method where possible. Bank/UPI may take a few business days.

5. Refund may be declined if

  • Request is after 7 days
  • Access was delivered and used without genuine technical issue
  • Sharing/reselling or policy violation
  • Wrong details submitted by user after access was available

6. Cancellation

You may stop using the service anytime. Cancellation does not auto-mean refund unless this policy is met.
