Cancellation and Refunds Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

7-day refund window for genuine issues.
If you face a valid problem within 7 (seven) days of successful payment / access activation, you may request a refund as described below.

1. Nature of product

GMAX Hub sells digital subscription access. Once access has been successfully delivered, the service is considered provided. There is no physical product to return.

2. Cancellation by customer

You may stop using the service at any time. Cancellation of use does not automatically mean a refund unless the request meets this policy.

If payment was made but you want to cancel before access is activated, contact us as soon as possible with your payment reference. We will review the request in good faith.

3. When refunds may be approved

Within 7 days of successful payment confirmation or access activation, refund requests may be considered for genuine issues such as:

  • Payment was successful but digital access was not delivered after reasonable verification
  • A technical problem on our side prevented use of the paid plan
  • Duplicate successful payment for the same order

4. How to request a refund or cancellation

Email or message us via Contact Us within 7 days and include:

  • Full name and registered email
  • Plan name and payment date
  • Payment reference (Razorpay payment ID / order ID, or UTR)
  • Clear description of the issue

5. Review and processing

We will review eligible requests. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where possible. Bank / UPI / card networks may take additional business days after we initiate the refund.

6. When refunds may be declined

  • Request is made after 7 days
  • Access was delivered and used without a genuine technical failure on our side
  • Sharing, reselling, or policy violation
  • Incorrect details provided by the user after access was available
  • Change of mind after substantial use of the digital content, except where required by law

7. Cancellation by us

We may cancel an order or access in cases of suspected fraud, chargeback abuse, or Terms violations. Where appropriate, a refund may be issued at our discretion.

8. Contact

For cancellation or refund requests: Contact Us.
