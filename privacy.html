Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GMAX Hub (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use instgmax.vercel.app.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number (if you provide it on forms)
  • Payment-related references (for example UTR, order ID, payment ID from the payment gateway)
  • Plan, amount, and selected profile
  • Device or browser identifiers needed to deliver and secure digital access
  • Basic usage data (such as page views) for operations and analytics

Payment card details, if any, are processed by the payment gateway (for example Razorpay) and are not stored by us on our servers as full card data.

2. How we use information

  • To process payments and deliver digital subscription access
  • To verify orders and prevent fraud
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve website performance and content
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Legal basis / voluntary provision

You provide personal details voluntarily when submitting forms or making a purchase. Without required details, we may be unable to complete your order or provide support.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including secure hosting and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please keep unlock codes and access private.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Payment gateways and banks to process payments
  • Hosting, database, and operational service providers who help us run the website
  • Authorities when required by law

6. Cookies and local storage

We may use cookies or local/session storage for session state, theme preference, profile selection, pending payment status, and similar functional needs.

7. Data retention

We retain order and access records as needed for service delivery, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance, and then delete or anonymise where appropriate.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your information, or raise privacy questions, via our Contact Us page.

9. Children’s privacy

The service is intended for users who can lawfully purchase digital services. If you believe a minor has provided data inappropriately, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address it.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date on this page will be revised when changes are published.

11. Contact

Privacy-related requests: Contact Us.
